Hand gestures

  • Pinch (thumb + index) on a button: click
  • Pinch on empty space: open or close the Home menu
  • Pinch and hold the title bar, then move: drag a window
  • Pinch and hold the green corner: resize
  • Pinch with both hands, then spread or close: scale a window
  • Open palm for 1 second: Home menu
  • Fist for 1.2 seconds: close the front window
  • You can also use your finger on the screen as a cursor
